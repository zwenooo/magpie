Magpie fork bundled Cursor backend
=================================

This distribution includes a locally built, modified headless backend derived
from cursor-byok (https://github.com/leookun/cursor-byok), upstream commit
31e9f5cbc98a7d28cffcb5efbb9323cd3e143354. Its source and local changes are recorded in
native/cursor-backend/UPSTREAM.md in this fork's source repository.
Cursor BYOK's desktop application is not required or downloaded at runtime.
